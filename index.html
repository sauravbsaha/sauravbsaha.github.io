About

Bridging fundamental science and therapeutic innovation

I am a Fellow of the Royal Society of Biology (FRSB) with more than 16 years of experience across computational biology, bioinformatics and translational research in both academia and industry.

As a founding member of the bioinformatics capability at Sygnature Discovery, I have helped establish and advance computational biology expertise within the organisation — building analytical pipelines, best-practice frameworks and target identification strategies that integrate cheminformatics, biosciences, medicinal chemistry and machine learning.

My earlier academic work focused on antimicrobial resistance and bacterial genomics, producing openly available resources — uCARE and uCAREChemSuiteCLI — that are used by researchers worldwide and cited in journals including Nature Reviews Genetics and Scientific Reports.

Today my work sits at the intersection of bioinformatics, machine learning and explainable AI, applied to target identification and prioritisation across oncology, metabolic disorders and neuroscience.
