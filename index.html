About

At Sygnature Discovery, I lead bioinformatics activities within cross-functional teams, developing and integrating scalable analytical workflows that support data-driven target discovery and drug discovery programmes.

My work sits at the intersection of Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Data Science, Cheminformatics and Artificial Intelligence, enabling biological insight generation and target prioritisation.

I helped establish and expand the Bioinformatics capability at Sygnature Discovery through scientific leadership, mentoring and workflow innovation.

Prior to industry, I led academic research in antimicrobial resistance (AMR), bacterial genomics and computational biology, developing widely used resources including uCARE and uCAREChemSuiteCLI.

I am a Fellow of the Royal Society of Biology (FRSB) and an invited expert participant at the European Commission Joint Research Centre Workshop on Antimicrobial Resistance Databases.
