About

Bioinformatics Lead specialising in Target Identification and computational drug discovery.

Working at the intersection of Biology, Cheminformatics, Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence, I develop computational strategies that accelerate target identification, prioritisation, and early discovery research.

At Sygnature Discovery, I collaborate closely with multidisciplinary teams across Oncology, Neuroscience, Metabolic Disease, and Immunology programmes, building explainable human‑in‑the‑loop approaches that support confident scientific decision making.

I helped establish and grow the Bioinformatics capability at Sygnature Discovery, contributing to strategy development, mentoring scientists, and strengthening computational expertise across the organisation.

Prior to industry, I led academic research in antimicrobial resistance (AMR), bacterial genomics and computational biology, developing widely used resources including uCARE and uCAREChemSuiteCLI.

Fellow of the Royal Society of Biology (FRSB) and invited expert participant at the European Commission JRC Workshop on Antimicrobial Resistance Databases.
